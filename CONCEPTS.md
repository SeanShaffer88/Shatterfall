## The Brute (AD2 — crimson embers, charred plate)

> A towering broad-shouldered warrior in charred obsidian-black layered plate armor with jagged scale and bone edges, an angular horned-crested helm, and bright crimson ember light glowing intensely from the cracks between armor plates as if the armor's inner fire powers a magical shield (brighter glow across chest and shoulders). He grips a massive two-handed greataxe with a smoldering crimson-hot blade edge. Palette: charcoal black, ash gray, crimson `#D92638` accents.

**AD checks:** ember glow = Crimson Guard charge (AD2); horned crest silhouette (AD1); warm crimson only (AD5).

## The Rogue (AD3 — angular light plate, gold trim, curved daggers)

> A lithe, agile skirmisher in sleek angular lightweight chrome and bronze plate over gunmetal-dark fabric underlayers — light, sharp silhouette contrasting deliberately with a heavy tank archetype, absolutely no spiked heavy armor or skulls. Elegant gold-bronze trim edges glow faintly amber along the armor seams, and thin trim pieces on the forearms flash with brief amber light. She wields two elongated curved daggers shaped like scimitars, held low in a ready stance, with a compact shortbow slung across her back. Palette: gunmetal dark blue-gray, bronze, amber `#E8912D` accents.

**AD checks:** no heavy spiked armor/skulls (AD3); gunmetal + gold warm accents (AD5); curved daggers distinct from the Brute's cleave; silhouette light-vs-mass contrast with the Brute (AD1).

## The Magi (AD4 — crescent robes, angular headdress, orb staff)

> A tall commanding sorceress in layered dark violet robes flaring outward in wide crescent shapes at the hem, wearing a broad angular low-swept headdress for maximum top-down silhouette readability, with stained-glass violet crystal panels embedded in her shoulder mantle. She holds a tall staff crowned with a glowing amber glass orb radiating warm light — the orb is the beam origin for her homing prism bolts, and thin translucent arcane glass panes float briefly around her like a hexagonal barrier. Palette: deep violet `#8F4FD4`, black, amber `#E8912D` orb glow.

**AD checks:** wide low headdress = top-down readability (AD1); orb glow = barrier state tie (AD4); violet/amber warm accents (AD5).
